    Meeting: questions before we start¶

    Prepared 2026-10-19. One list of everything we need to decide before building the real website and opening the salon.

    How to use this page in the meeting

    • Go through the sections in order. Section A blocks everything else, so do it first.
    • For each question, write down the answer, who takes it on, and by when.
    • After the meeting, answers move into Decisions, Business facts or the topic pages, and the question is removed here.
    • Sensitive answers (partner shares, money, budgets, personal data) are not written on this public site. They go into the private notes (private/).

    A. Business & legal (blocking)¶

    • 1. Salon name. What is the final name?
      • Why: needed for the commercial register, the .ch domain, Instagram/Facebook handles and the logo.
      • Next step: check availability in Zefix, for the .ch domain and for the social handles.
    • 2. Partners and roles. Who are the partners, and who does what?
      • Roles to cover: therapist, website & tech, marketing & social media, admin & bookkeeping, client communication.
      • Shares and money: private notes only.
    • 3. Legal form. Sole proprietorship (Einzelunternehmen), partnership (Kollektivgesellschaft) or GmbH?
      • Depends on question 2 and on liability. See Business setup.
    • 4. Therapist. Qualifications and diplomas, recognition of a foreign diploma if needed, and a work/residence permit that allows this work.
    • 5. Fiduciary (Treuhänder). Do we use one for registration, bookkeeping and a legal check of the website before launch? Who?
    • 6. Bank and payments. Business bank account, TWINT for business, card terminal (e.g. SumUp) or not?
    • 7. Insurance. Professional liability (Berufshaftpflicht), contents/premises insurance, accident insurance.
    • 8. VAT (MWST). Do we expect less than CHF 100,000 turnover a year (no VAT needed), or do we register voluntarily?

    B. Premises¶

    • 9. Address. What is the real address? Dorfstrasse 1, 3792 Saanen in the prototype is only a sample.
    • 10. Use of the premises. Is commercial use allowed? Do we need a change-of-use permit? Can we put up a sign?
    • 11. Parking. Which car parks do we recommend to clients? Are there private spaces? Fees and payment methods [verify] on site.
    • 12. Opening date. Target date, and what must be ready by then (equipment, linen, oils, furniture)?

    C. Service, prices and rules¶

    • 13. Services at launch. Only the anti-cellulite massage? Which services may come later?
    • 14. Durations. Trial session 45 min and regular session 60 min, or different?
    • 15. Prices. Final prices for the trial session, a single session and the packages.
      • Note: the prototype shows CHF 130 for 60 min, but the Dikidi account shows CHF 100. One price everywhere.
    • 16. Packages. 5 and 10 sessions? How long are they valid? Paid in advance?
    • 17. Cancellation and no-shows. ✅ Decided 2026-10-24: no cancellation or no-show fees, no deposit (cash only). Clients are asked to cancel as early as possible.
    • 18. Opening hours. Days and hours, seasonal changes, holidays and closures.
    • 19. Languages. Which languages does the therapist speak with clients?
    • 20. Gift vouchers. At launch or later? Sold online, in the studio, or both?
    • 21. Payment in the studio. ✅ Decided 2026-10-24: cash only at launch, no online prepayment.
    • 22. Client health questions. Do we ask about contraindications (pregnancy, varicose veins, recent surgery…) before the first session?
      • Why: health data is sensitive personal data under Swiss data protection law. If we collect it, we need a clear process: paper or digital, where it is stored, who can see it.

    D. Booking tool¶

    • 23. Which tool? ✅ Decided 2026-10-24: Cal.com. See Booking tool: buy or build?.
      • Open: the booking screen follows the visitor's browser language. Do we want one Cal.com event type per language, so it always matches the page? See Booking calendar language.
      • Criteria: booking screens in DE/EN/FR/IT, a "Book" button inside Instagram/Facebook, booking from Google Maps, cost, ease of use for the therapist.
      • Test result so far: Cal.com follows the visitor's language. Dikidi opened in English, showed one Russian label ("Без категории", "No category") and its own cookie banner.
    • 24. Account owner. In whose name is the account, and which email and phone are used?
    • 25. Reminders. Email only, or also SMS/WhatsApp reminders (may cost extra)?
    • 26. Data we ask for at booking. Minimum: name, phone, email? Anything else?
    • 27. Public profile. What may be shown in the booking window: the therapist's name, photo, location?

    E. Website¶

    • 28. Technology. Simple static site (like the prototype), Rails, or a site builder? Who maintains it?
    • 29. Domain. Which .ch domain? Also .swiss and common misspellings? Registered in the company's name, with which registrar? Who holds the login?
    • 30. Email. Business addresses on the domain (e.g. info@, booking@)? Which provider?
    • 31. Design. Keep the prototype's style and colours? Who designs the logo?
    • 32. Photos. Professional photoshoot of the studio and the treatment? (No before/after photos; see the ad rules in Marketing.)
    • 33. Texts and translations. Who writes the texts, and who approves them? Who translates into DE/FR/IT (native speakers, not machine translation, especially for the legal pages)?
    • 34. More languages later. e.g. RU, UK, AR, ZH: which ones, and when?
    • 35. Legal pages. Impressum data, privacy policy and cookie banner. Who checks them before launch (fiduciary or lawyer)?

    F. Marketing¶

    • 36. Social accounts. Which do we create (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp Business), with which handles? Who posts, and how often?
    • 37. Google Business Profile. Who owns and manages it? Verification needs the real address (by postcard or video).
    • 38. WhatsApp number. Which phone number is the business number? It should be able to move to the WhatsApp Business API later.
    • 39. Ad budget. Monthly budget per season (private notes only). See the plan in Marketing.
    • 40. Who runs the ads? Ourselves (recommended) or a freelancer for the first setup? How many hours per week?
    • 41. Local partners. Which hotels, chalet agencies, gyms and salons do we contact? Who contacts them? What do we offer (commission, free session, cards with a QR code)?
    • 42. Opening offer. A special trial-session price or an opening event?
    • 43. Reviews. How do we ask clients for Google reviews (a card with a QR code, a message after the visit)?

    G. Later (Phase 2)¶

    • 44. Booking from WhatsApp and AI assistants. When do we start? See Phase 2: Booking everywhere.
    • 45. A second therapist. At what point (bookings per week, waiting list) do we hire?

    After the meeting¶

    • Answers moved into Decisions, Business facts and the topic pages
    • Sensitive answers written into private/
    • Each open task has a person and a date
    • Answered questions removed from this page and from Open questions
